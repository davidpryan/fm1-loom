## Not done, and why

- The wavetables (`WT`, 36,864 B, the largest symbol): four of the nine tables are odd-symmetric in every frame, so
  half of each could be stored (8 KB). The read in `wt_run` would cost a few instructions more a sample, four times a
  sample: it needs the voice count measured on an FM-1 first.
- The fonts (19.5 KB): 4-bit alphas already trimmed to their ink boxes and run-coded. fm1-base's Huffman packing
  (`font_pack`) would take about a third more, at three times the instructions for text, or RAM for a glyph cache.
- `ram_layout = "packed"` (fm1-base): one SRAM budget instead of three regions. It moves every RAM address; not
  without an FM-1 to test on.
